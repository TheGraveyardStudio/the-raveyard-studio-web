Privacy Policy — Hobbe Baby

Company: Hobbe Baby • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hobbe Baby ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide baby products, nursery essentials, and e-commerce services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, social media integrations (including TikTok), and online stores.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you connect or interact through third-party platforms (such as TikTok), we may receive basic authorized profile or content metadata strictly in accordance with the permissions you grant.
  • Customer & Order Information: Name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and phone number provided when placing an order.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and cookies used for site functionality and analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill and deliver baby product orders and provide customer support,
  • To manage and publish authorized social media content and product showcases,
  • To improve our products, website performance, and user experience,
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and consumer protection regulations.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with trusted service providers (such as shipping carriers, payment processors, and authorized platform APIs) solely to perform services on our behalf.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data. We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
